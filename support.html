Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my library stored?

On your Mac and, if you use iCloud sync with Slate Box Pro, in your private iCloud storage. We do not have servers that receive your library.

How do I back up my library?

Choose Export in the app menu and save a JSON file. You can bring it back later with Import, which is free. Export is part of Slate Box Pro.

What does Slate Box Pro include?

Unlimited folders and tags, export to JSON and CSV, and iCloud sync between your Macs. The free plan has no limit on games and movies and includes up to 3 folders and 5 tags.

I bought Pro on another Mac. How do I get it back?

Open Slate Box Pro from the app menu and click "Restore Purchases", signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the App Store, click your name, then Account Settings, and manage Subscriptions. Pro stays active until the end of the paid period.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

Introduction

Mikhail Gudzikevich ("we", "our", "us") operates Slate Box, a Mac app for keeping a personal list of games and movies with your ratings, statuses, notes, folders and tags. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored and who else may receive it.

In short: your library stays on your Mac and, if you turn on iCloud sync, in your own private iCloud storage. We do not run servers, show ads or use analytics, and we never see your library.

Information Stored on Your Mac

  • Your library: game and movie titles, ratings, statuses, notes, dates, folders, tags, cover images you add, and game details such as genres, platform, developers and release date.
  • Your settings, such as sorting and filters.

This information is stored on your Mac using Apple's on-device storage. You can edit or delete any entry at any time. Deleting the app's data removes your library from the Mac. If Time Machine or another backup is enabled, it may include this data, which is controlled by you.

iCloud Sync (Apple)

  • With Slate Box Pro you can sync your library between your Macs signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • Synced data is stored in your private iCloud database, which is provided by Apple and is not accessible to us.
  • This processing is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. You can turn iCloud off for the app in System Settings.

Purchases (Apple)

  • Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details.
  • The app checks with Apple on your Mac whether you own Slate Box Pro, so it can unlock Pro features.

Export and Import

When you export your library, the file is saved only where you choose. When you import a file, it is read only on your Mac.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not require accounts, logins or email addresses.
  • We do not operate servers that receive your library.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or crash reporting services.
  • The app does not track you across apps and websites.
  • The app does not access your contacts, location, camera or microphone. Cover images are read only from files you choose.
  • We do not have access to your payment information.

Sharing and Sale of Information

We do not sell or share your personal information. The only third party involved is Apple, for iCloud sync and purchases, as described above.

Data Retention

  • Your library stays on your Mac and in your iCloud until you delete it.
  • We do not keep copies of your data.

Your Rights

Your library is under your control: you can view, correct, export and delete it in the app at any time. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you also have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing and to data portability, and you may complain to your local data protection authority. Because we do not receive your data, most requests can be fulfilled directly in the app; for information held by Apple, you can exercise your rights with Apple.

Data Security

Your library is protected by your Mac's security and, for iCloud sync, by your Apple Account. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

The app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
